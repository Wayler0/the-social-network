# Round 2: real-phone test (5 students, 20 minutes each)

**Issue:** #44 · **Due:** Sun 29 Nov · **Who runs it:** Solomon + Peter (one asks, one takes notes)
**Who:** 5 students who have **not** used the app yet, on **their own phones**:
- at least 2 cheap Android phones;
- at least 1 iPhone, if possible;
- at least 1 fresher (18+).

**Where:** run 2 sessions on campus Wi-Fi and 3 on mobile data. Note which one each session used.

**Goal:** find what stops a real student on a real phone. We are not checking whether they like it.

## Before you start (say this)

> "We're testing a campus app the team is building. We're testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that's our mistake. Please think out loud. It takes about 20 minutes. I won't write down your name, and you can stop at any time. Is that okay?"

- Continue only if they say yes. If they are under 18, thank them and stop.
- Don't help unless they are stuck for more than a minute. Write down where they got stuck.

## Tasks (read each one out; don't show them where to tap)

| # | Task | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Open **hatua.nextup.co.ke** and join with your student email." | Do they understand "campus email"? **Time from Send code to the email arriving.** Was it in the inbox or in spam? Did they leave the browser to check email and come back? |
| 2 | "Set up your profile." | Course: did they pick a suggestion or type their own? Did they skip the reg number (it's optional)? |
| 3 | "Find your classmates." | Do they find their class group? Is the right group shown? Do they follow anyone? |
| 4 | "Post a photo from your gallery." | Does the photo editor make sense (shape, zoom, Use photo)? **How long the upload takes on mobile data.** |
| 5 | "You made a typo. Fix it." | Do they find **··· → Edit post**? |
| 6 | "Add a story." | Background colours, audience (Followers or Close friends). |
| 7 | "Find a person or group you know." | Do they use the search icon on Home, or Discover? |
| 8 | "Ask a senior where the library is, without your name showing." | Do they find **Hide my name from other students**? |
| 9 | "Put the app on your home screen." | The install banner, or the browser menu. iPhone: did they find Share → Add to Home Screen? |
| 10 | "Close the app and open it again from the home screen." | Are they **still signed in**? (They should stay signed in for ~6 months.) |

## Last 3 questions

1. The app is **dark by default**. Do you like it? Can you read everything, including outdoors in sunlight?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. Would you open this again tomorrow? Why or why not?
