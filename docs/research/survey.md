# Week 0 anonymous form — questions

Google Form, ~3 minutes, shared in class groups and clubs. **Anonymous:** email collection OFF, no names, no sign-in required.

**Intro text:** We're Tharaka University students building Hatua, a campus app for students, staff and alumni. This short form (about 3 minutes) helps us build what you actually need. It's anonymous and voluntary — we don't collect your name or email. Thank you!

| # | Question | Type | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are you 18 or older? | Multiple choice, required | Yes · No *(No → thank-you page, form ends)* |
| 2 | Which best describes you? | Multiple choice, required | Year 1 student · Year 2 student · Year 3 student · Year 4+ student · Postgraduate student · Staff · Alumni |
| 3 | Where do you get campus news today? | Checkboxes | WhatsApp class/course groups · Official university email · Student portal · Notice boards · Friends · Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/X · Other |
| 4 | How often do you miss something important (deadline, notice, event) because you didn't hear about it in time? | Linear scale 1–5 | 1 = Never … 5 = Very often |
| 5 | How interested are you in each of these? | Grid (rows × columns) | Rows: Campus news from official offices · Ask a Senior (questions answered by senior students) · Groups for my course and year · Club pages and announcements · Campus events · Campus market (buy and sell) · Private chat · Short campus videos · Campus map · Job and internship posts. Columns: Would use a lot · Maybe · Not interested |
| 6 | What frustrates you most about WhatsApp class groups? | Paragraph, optional | — |
| 7 | What could stop you from joining a campus app? | Checkboxes | Privacy worries · Data/bundle cost · Phone storage · Lecturers or staff seeing my posts · Already on too many apps · Nothing — I'd join · Other |
| 8 | Your phone | Multiple choice | Android · iPhone · Other |
| 9 | How do you mostly get online on campus? | Multiple choice | Campus Wi-Fi · Mobile data · Both equally |
| 10 | Would you like to test an early version for 10 minutes? | Multiple choice | Yes · No *(we don't collect contacts here — if yes, tell the person who shared this form)* |
| 11 | Anything else you'd like a campus app to do? | Paragraph, optional | — |

**Thank-you text:** Thanks for helping build Hatua! If you'd like to test an early version, message the person who shared this form.
