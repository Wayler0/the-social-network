# Week 0 anonymous form — questions

Google Form "Hatua campus survey (Week 0)", ~3 minutes, shared in class groups and clubs.
Live link: https://forms.gle/UzhKWHRRcZrSEhQb8 (owner: WaylerTech Google account).
**Anonymous:** email collection OFF, no names, no sign-in required, "submit another response" link OFF.
This file mirrors the live form — update it whenever the form changes.

**Title:** Hatua: help us build your campus app

**Intro text:** We're Tharaka students building Hatua, one app for our campus community: campus news and official notices, groups for your course and year, club pages, and an "Ask a Senior" space where seniors answer your questions. Hatua is independent; it's not an official university app. Before we build it, we want to know what you actually need. It takes about 3 minutes, it's anonymous and voluntary, and we don't collect your name or email. Thank you!

**Section 1** — question 1 only. **Section 2: About you and campus life** — questions 2–11.

| # | Question | Type | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are you 18 or older? | Multiple choice, **required** | Yes · No *(No → form submits and ends)* |
| 2 | Which best describes you? | Multiple choice, **required** | Year 1 student · Year 2 student · Year 3 student · Year 4+ student · Postgraduate student · Staff · Alumni (former student) |
| 3 | Where do you get campus news today? | Checkboxes | WhatsApp class/course groups · Official university email · Student portal · Notice boards · Friends · Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / X · Other |
| 4 | How often do you miss something important (deadline, notice, event) because you didn't hear about it in time? | Linear scale 1–5 | 1 = Never … 5 = Very often |
| 5 | Which of these would you actually use? Tick all that apply. | Checkboxes | Campus news from official offices · Ask a Senior (questions answered by senior students) · Groups for my course and year · Club pages and announcements · Campus events · Campus market (buy and sell) · Private chat · Short campus videos · Campus map · Job and internship posts |
| 6 | What annoys you most about WhatsApp class groups? | Paragraph | — |
| 7 | What could stop you from joining a campus app? | Checkboxes | Privacy worries · Data/bundle cost · Phone storage · Lecturers or staff seeing my posts · Already on too many apps · Nothing, I'd join · Other |
| 8 | What kind of phone do you use? | Multiple choice | Android · iPhone · Other |
| 9 | How do you mostly get online on campus? | Multiple choice | Wi-Fi/ campus WiFi · Mobile data · Both equally |
| 10 | Would you like to test an early version for 10 minutes? (If yes, tell the person who shared this form. We don't collect contacts here.) | Multiple choice, **required** | Yes · No |
| 11 | Anything else you'd like a campus app to do? | Paragraph | — |

**Thank-you text:** Thanks for helping build Hatua! If you'd like to test an early version, message the person who shared this form.
