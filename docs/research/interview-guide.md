# Week 0 interview guide (10 minutes)

**Who:** 8–10 people — freshers (18+), continuing students (different schools and years), 1–2 staff, 1 alumnus. Each team member does about 3.
**Goal:** learn what people really need before we lock v0.1. Listen more than you talk; don't pitch Hatua until the end.

## Before you start (say this)

> "We're students building a campus app for Tharaka University. I'd like to ask you a few questions about how you get campus information — about 10 minutes. There are no right answers. I won't write down your name, and you can stop at any time. Is that okay?"

Only continue if they say yes. If they're under 18, thank them and stop.

## Questions

1. What year and course are you in? *(or: role, for staff/alumni)*
2. Tell me about the last time you **missed something important** on campus — a deadline, a notice, an event. What happened?
3. Where do you get campus news today? Which source do you trust most? Why?
4. What's good and what's annoying about your **WhatsApp class groups**?
5. When you have a question about a course, a lecturer or campus life, **who do you ask**? How does that go?
6. *(Freshers)* What was hardest in your first weeks? What do you wish someone had told you?
7. Do you buy or sell things on campus (books, phones, services)? How?
8. How do you use your phone for school — Wi-Fi or bundles? How much data do you spend in a week?
9. What would make you **not** join a new campus app? *(privacy, data cost, staff seeing posts, too many apps…)*
10. *Now show the canvas or describe Hatua in one sentence.* What's your first reaction? Which part would you use first? What's missing?

## After (within an hour)

Write anonymous notes in `docs/research/notes/` (one file per person, e.g. `2026-11-14-year1-bsc-cs.md`):
- Who (year/course or role — no name)
- 3 most important things they said (quote short phrases)
- Surprises
- Ideas for Hatua

Bring the top findings to the Sunday briefing. Anything that should change the plan becomes a GitHub issue labelled `user-feedback`.
